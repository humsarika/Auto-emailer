Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name and email.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information to respond to your inquiries and improve our services.

3. Data Security

Your data is completely safe and protected with us. We do not store any of your uploaded files (CSV or resume) permanently — they are only used temporarily during the email process and are automatically deleted once the process is complete.

Your login passwords are securely hashed and encrypted in our database, meaning we cannot access or view your actual password at any point.

Important Note: To send emails on your behalf, we recommend using an App Password instead of your main email password. App Passwords are specially generated by your email provider (like Gmail) to allow secure access to your account without exposing your main password. This ensures an extra layer of security and control for you.

We never store or misuse any of your email credentials. Your trust and security are our top priority.
